ENGINEERING CONTEXT

Custom FDM 3D-printed PLA fixtures were used as practical metrology tooling to position components consistently during CMM inspection. The supports were shaped around the component geometry so that the part could be loaded repeatably without unnecessarily covering inspection surfaces.

For CMM work, the fixture is part of the measurement setup: a stable, repeatable location reduces setup variation, while open support geometry preserves probe access. The objective is controlled restraint rather than simply maximising clamping force.

METROLOGY DESIGN CONSIDERATIONS
01Repeatable location - define a consistent loading position so the inspection routine starts from the same physical condition.
02Constraint control - locate securely without over-constraining a part that could deflect or distort under restraint.
03Probe access - keep contact points away from measured surfaces and preserve practical probe approach paths.
04Fixture validation - confirm fit, seating and repeatable loading before relying on the fixture for inspection.

WHY FDM PLA IS USEFUL HERE
01Geometry freedom - FDM can produce customised support shapes without conventional machining of complex contours.
02Rapid iteration - fixture geometry can be modified and reprinted as the component or inspection requirement changes.
03Low-mass tooling - polymer supports are practical for lightweight inspection workholding and quick handling.
04Process sensitivity - PLA print accuracy depends on parameters such as layer height, orientation and thermal conditions, so the fixture should be checked after manufacture.
05Inspection-focused design - the value comes from repeatable positioning and access to the measurement surfaces, not simply from holding the part tightly.
