RANGE OF INSPECTION GEOMETRY
CMM programming required the inspection strategy to be adapted to the component's geometry, feature density and accessibility. Across large annular components, rotational parts and compact feature-dense components, programming involved establishing appropriate datum references, selecting measurable features, planning probe approaches and sequencing the inspection to obtain reliable dimensional and geometric results.
FEATURE-BASED INSPECTION
The program was structured around measurable geometric features rather than simply probe movements: planes, cylinders, circles, holes, bores, diameters, annular surfaces, feature centres and feature-to-feature relationships. Their evaluation was driven by the drawing and applicable GD&T requirements; the photographs show representative component geometries, not a claim that every listed feature was measured on every part.
