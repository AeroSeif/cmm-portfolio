FIXTURE VALIDATION
& MEASUREMENT REPEATABILITY
The objective was not maximum clamping force. The workholding condition had to prevent movement under probing while avoiding excessive constraint that could alter measured geometry. Repeated loading and measurement trials were used to assess setup behaviour and refine locator positions/restraint before documenting the loading condition for operators.

EXCESSIVE CONSTRAINT

Clamping-induced distortion → altered workpiece geometry.

→

OPTIMISED CONSTRAINT

Stable workpiece → preserved geometry → repeatable measurement.

→

INSUFFICIENT CONSTRAINT

Workpiece slippage under probing force → point-to-point displacement → reduced repeatability.

01 / PROBE-INDUCED DISPLACEMENT

When a circular feature is acquired from multiple touch points, the CMM calculates the feature from those coordinates. If the workpiece moves between contacts, the points may no longer represent one stationary physical position.

02 / POSITION & CIRCULARITY

For holes with position tolerances, movement can affect the reported feature location relative to the established datum reference frame. Circularity remains a separate form control.

03 / CONTROLLED CONTACT

Locator position, contact condition and workpiece elevation were treated as part of the measurement strategy, balancing stability, accessibility and the risk of clamping-induced distortion.

04 / SETUP REPRODUCIBILITY

The selected arrangement was documented through photographs and operator instructions so the component could be loaded consistently without claiming perfect repeatability or eliminating all variation.
